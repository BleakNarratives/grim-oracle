# Branding prompts

Copy-paste text for image tools (Bing Image Creator, Gemini, Midjourney, whatever).
Each block is self-contained: palette, composition, and what to avoid.

Design constraints these encode, from the existing mark:

- Palette: near-black ink `#0c0a07`, arcane gold `#b8972f`, phosphor teal `#6dffb3`, bone `#ede5d4`.
- Type: Roman stone-cut caps (Cinzel-like) for the wordmark, monospace for anything technical.
- Motif: an open grimoire whose spine is a git merge arrow; two pages labelled INTENDED and EXECUTED.
- Lineage: 90s/00s skate deck graphics, reread for 2026 developer tooling.
- Avoid: glossy 3D, purple AI gradients, robots, brains, neural-net clichés, lens flares.

---

## 1 — Mark, flat vector (primary)

> A flat vector emblem, strict two-color linework on a warm near-black field (#0c0a07): an open grimoire seen front-on, its spine rendered as a git merge arrow pointing upward in phosphor teal (#6dffb3). The left page reads INTENDED, the right page EXECUTED, set in tiny monospace caps. A thin arcane-gold circle (#b8972f) frames the book like a wax seal. Symmetrical, centered, no gradients, no glow, no 3D. Thick outer stroke so it reads at 32 pixels. Screenprint ink texture.

## 2 — Mark, seal variation

> A circular wax-seal badge stamped in bone white (#ede5d4) on near-black. Inside, an open book seen from slightly above, spine as a merge arrow, gold ring border with a hairline inner rule. Weathered letterpress edge, like a rubber stamp on a gig flyer. Flat, high contrast, one accent color only.

## 3 — Hero image, argument-against-yourself

> A dark editorial poster: a single pitch document on a black desk, lit hard from one side. Over it, a chalk outline of a debate in phosphor teal, two arrows meeting head-on and one refusing to merge. Arcane gold annotations in the margins, hand-scrawled in monospace. 90s skate-deck composition: heavy black border, small caps headline reading GRIM ORACLE, a stamped verdict block reading NEEDS-REVIEW. Grain, ink texture, no photographic faces, no 3D.

## 4 — Product screenshot mock (for posts)

> A terminal window floating on near-black with a bone-white monospace transcript, showing a debate report: PROPONENT, OPPONENT, JUDGE, then a DISSENT block with a single hard objection. A phosphor teal cursor. To the right, a small stamp reading SIMULATED in gold. Clean, legible at thumbnail size, no chrome reflections, no drop shadows.

## 5 — Cannibal mode cover

> A 90s hardcore flyer for a fictional event called CANNIBAL MODE: photocopied black and bone white, one phosphor teal accent, distressed halftone. Bold condensed caps. The focal image is an open book being torn down its spine-arrow, the two halves disagreeing. Xerox streaks, staple marks, nothing clean.

## 6 — Icon set (UI)

> Six flat monochrome icons on transparent background, 2px stroke, phosphor teal: a book with an arrow spine, a speech bubble with a crack through it, a stamped seal, a scale tilted to one side, a magnifying glass over a quoted sentence, a lock with a keyhole shaped like a cursor. Geometric, no rounded cutesy corners, consistent optical weight.
